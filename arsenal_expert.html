1. Arsenal FC – Brief History

▼

This section tells you the brief history of Arsenal, what kind of team it was, and its recent transformations. (So when your friend mentioned about it, at least you can say something...)

Founded

1886 in Woolwich, South London, as Dial Square (later Woolwich Arsenal)

Expression: "Do you know that Arsenal was not located in North London in the beginning, but in South London?"

Move to North London

1913, relocating to Highbury; became Arsenal FC.

Early Dominance

1930s under manager Herbert Chapman—won multiple league titles and FA Cups; pioneered tactical innovations and modern club management.

Post-war Success

Continued domestic success, including league and FA Cup wins in the 1950s–70s.

Double & Invincibles

1970–71: First league and FA Cup "Double."

Defensive Style

In the 1980s to 90s, under managers like George Graham, Arsenal was known for tight defenses and winning with few goals (e.g., 1-0), a strategy that frustrated opponents but brought success. People started to call Arsenal "The Boring Arsenal".

Expression: "With the wonderful players in our current squad, we have both the good defence as in the 'boring Arsenal' period, and the sharp attack as in Wenger period."

2003–04: Went unbeaten in the Premier League ("The Invincibles").

Expression: "Invincibles. Unbeaten. Never done before, never done since."
Expression: "Highbury, Wenger, the Invincibles -- put some respect on our name."

Arsène Wenger Era (1996–2018)

Modernized the club; 3 league titles, 7 FA Cups, consistent Champions League qualification.

Emirates Stadium

Moved from Highbury in 2006.

Recent Years

Focus on rebuilding and youth; multiple FA Cups in the 2010s and renewed title contention in the 2020s.

Expression: "Thanks to Arsène Wenger's vision on developing our own youth camps, we now have so many talented youth players of our own. Nwaneri, Max Dowman, Lewis Skelly, Saka... you name it."

Honours Highlights

  • 13 English league titles
  • 14 FA Cups (record)
Expression: "Most FA Cups — that's not up for debate."

1. Tottenham Hotspur (Spurs) 🐔 – The North London Derby Rival

▼
Quick Facts About Spurs
  • Nickname: "Spurs" or "The Lilywhites"
  • Stadium: Tottenham Hotspur Stadium (62,850 capacity)
  • Last Major Trophy: League Cup 2008 (17+ years ago!)
  • Famous For: Bottling leads, "trophy drought," Harry Kane leaving
  • Rivalry Since: 1887 (over 130 years!)
What To Say:
  • "It's the history of the Tottenham." (Classic meme)
  • "When did Spurs last win a trophy? I genuinely can't remember."
  • "North London is red, always has been."
  • "They had Harry Kane for 10 years and won nothing. Says it all."
  • "At least we won something this millennium."
Key Context: The North London Derby is Arsenal's biggest rivalry. Tottenham hasn't won a Premier League title, and their last major trophy was in 2008. Arsenal fans love reminding them of this—it's called "banter." When Arsenal beats Spurs, you'll hear: "What do we think of Tottenham? Shit! What do we think of shit? Tottenham!"

2. Chelsea FC 💰 – The West London Money Club

▼
Quick Facts About Chelsea
  • Nickname: "The Blues"
  • Stadium: Stamford Bridge (40,343 capacity)
  • Spending: Over £1 billion since Boehly takeover (2022)
  • Famous For: Endless spending, managerial carousel, rent boys chant
  • Recent Trophy: Champions League 2021
What To Say:
  • "Chelsea spent a billion and still can't beat us."
  • "They've got 40 players and no idea what to do with them."
  • "Remember when we beat them 5-0? Good times."
  • "Another manager sacked already? Classic Chelsea."
  • "All that money and they still need to loan out half the squad."
Key Context: Chelsea became a financial powerhouse after Roman Abramovich bought them in 2003. Under new owner Todd Boehly (since 2022), they've spent absurd amounts on players. Arsenal fans mock their "buying success" strategy and chaotic transfer policy. The rivalry intensified in the 2000s-2010s when both competed for top 4.

3. Manchester City 🤖 – The Oil Money Champions

▼
Quick Facts About Man City
  • Nickname: "City" or "The Citizens"
  • Stadium: Etihad Stadium (53,400 capacity—often empty seats)
  • Recent Success: 4 Premier League titles in a row (2020-2024)
  • Famous For: Financial Fair Play charges (115!), Pep Guardiola, Haaland
  • Arsenal Context: Direct title rivals recently (2022-23, 2023-24)
What To Say:
  • "115 charges and counting. Let's see what happens."
  • "Their stadium's emptier than Tottenham's trophy cabinet."
  • "We collapsed last season, but we'll bounce back. They won't last forever."
  • "All that money and they still can't fill their stadium."
  • "Infinite money cheat code activated."
Key Context: Man City became dominant after being bought by Sheikh Mansour in 2008. They face 115 Financial Fair Play charges. Arsenal challenged them for the 2022-23 and 2023-24 titles but fell short both times. This is a painful rivalry for Arsenal fans right now—but use it to show you're realistic, not just biased. Admitting City are good (but adding the "115 charges" caveat) makes you sound balanced.

4. Manchester United 😂 – The Fallen Giants

▼
Quick Facts About Man United
  • Nickname: "United" or "The Red Devils"
  • Stadium: Old Trafford (74,310 capacity—leaky roof)
  • Glory Days: Under Sir Alex Ferguson (retired 2013)
  • Current State: Mid-table, constant managerial changes, poor signings
  • Famous For: Living in the past, "We want Glazers out," Fred & McTominay
What To Say:
  • "Man United are back! ...to being mid-table."
  • "They're still living in the Ferguson era. It's been over 10 years, lads."
  • "Remember when they signed Antony for £86 million? Yeah."
  • "Their biggest achievement this season is beating us. That's it."
  • "Old Trafford roof leaks more than their defense."
Key Context: Man United dominated English football under Sir Alex Ferguson (1986-2013) but have struggled since. Arsenal fans enjoy watching them decline because of the intense rivalry during the late 90s and 2000s (Wenger vs Ferguson). They're now seen as overhyped and underperforming—easy banter material. However, they still occasionally beat Arsenal, so don't get too cocky.

5. Liverpool FC 🔴 – The Scouse Reds

▼
Quick Facts About Liverpool
  • Nickname: "The Reds" or "The Pool"
  • Stadium: Anfield (61,276 capacity)
  • Recent Success: Premier League 2020, Champions League 2019
  • Famous For: Jürgen Klopp, intense fans, "You'll Never Walk Alone"
  • Arsenal Context: Historic rivalry, title races in past
What To Say:
  • "Liverpool are great... when they're not finishing 5th."
  • "Klopp left because he couldn't handle another rebuild."
  • "They won one Premier League in 30 years and act like they're prime Barcelona."
  • "Remember when we beat them 3-1 at Anfield? Good times."
  • "They're loud, I'll give them that. Results? Not always."
Key Context: Liverpool are historically one of England's most successful clubs but had a 30-year wait for a Premier League title (1990-2020). Under Jürgen Klopp, they became elite again. The rivalry with Arsenal is historic, especially from the 1980s-2000s title battles. Klopp left in 2024, and new manager Arne Slot took over. Liverpool fans are passionate (some say insufferable), so banter here is fair game.

6. General Premier League Banter Tips & Other Teams

▼
How to Sound Like an Expert
Golden Rules of Football Banter:
  • Rule 1: Always mention trophy count. Arsenal have 13 league titles and 14 FA Cups (record).
  • Rule 2: Mock spending (especially Chelsea, City, United). Arsenal are "self-sustaining."
  • Rule 3: Remind Spurs they have no trophies since 2008. Never gets old.
  • Rule 4: When Arsenal lose: "It's a process. Trust Arteta." When Arsenal win: "We're winning the league!"
  • Rule 5: Always mention "Invincibles" (2003-04 unbeaten season). No one else has done it in Premier League.
  • Rule 6: If someone says Arsenal bottled it: "We're young. We'll bounce back." (Shows maturity)
Advanced Tip: Real experts balance banter with respect. Saying "City are incredible, but 115 charges is suspicious" sounds better than "City are cheats." Saying "Spurs have a nice stadium, shame about the trophy cabinet" is funnier than just "Spurs are shit." Mix humor with facts for maximum credibility.
Bonus: Other Teams to Know
  • Aston Villa: "Historically big, currently good under Emery. Respect, but we're bigger."
  • Newcastle: "Saudi money, nice fans, but no history of sustained success yet."
  • Brighton: "Well-run club. They sell their best players to us (White, Trossard). Feeder club."
  • West Ham: "Always competitive against us for some reason. Annoying but not a real rival."
  • Everton: "Mid-table mediocrity. Liverpool's little brother."
Generic Response for Any Team: "They're alright, but let's be honest—Arsenal are clear. 13 league titles, 14 FA Cups, and we're on the way back to the top."
