Welcome to Arsenal Squad 25/26

The author is a passionate Arsenal FC fan who started watching football 2 years ago, but compared to my knowledgeable roommate who followed Arsenal since childhood, I feel like a total beginner. This is exactly why I created this non-profit website. It provides everything you need to know about modern football and the Gunners.

Our Mission

Our goal is to help those who just started watching Arsenal and want to blend in with other Arsenal fans, by offering comprehensive information on the current squad, player profiles, and oral expressions for chatting.

What We Offer

  • Player Profiles: Detailed information about each Arsenal player including stats, position, and background.
  • Soccer Slang Dictionary: A comprehensive guide to football terminology and slang to help you sound like a true football expert.
  • Season Information: Access to information about different Arsenal seasons.
  • Multilingual Support: Our website supports multiple languages to serve fans worldwide.

Stay Connected

We are constantly updating our content to provide you with the most accurate and up-to-date information about Arsenal FC. Whether you're a lifelong Gooner or new to the beautiful game, we're here to help you understand and appreciate everything about Arsenal.
