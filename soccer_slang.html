Soccer Slang Dictionary

Have you watched a few football games and want to boast about your football knowledge in front of your friends? Or do you want to blend into the strong soccer atmosphere in your neighborhood by sounding more professional? Whatever it is, we've got you covered!

Below are over 200 soccer slang terms listed in categories, ensuring you can express yourself in every situation. You are welcome to edit or add slang terms that you know that are not on the list.
